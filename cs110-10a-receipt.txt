## R2 - Three engines and my choice

Unreal Engine / 2D and 3D / C++ and Blueprints / High-quality graphics and visual scripting / Official link: https://www.unrealengine.com/
Godot / 2D and 3D / GDScript and C# / Free, open-source, and beginner-friendly / Official link: https://godotengine.org/

I would try Godot because how easily accessible it is while being free and beginner- friendly.
